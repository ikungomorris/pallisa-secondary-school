ABOUT THE VIRTUAL LAB

Learn Science Through Practical Activities

The Virtual Laboratory provides learners with a digital environment where they can study practical concepts before, during or after physical laboratory sessions.

It can support Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Geography and ICT practical learning.

Learners should always follow teacher instructions and laboratory safety procedures.
