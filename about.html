WHO WE ARE

About Pallisa Secondary School

Pallisa Secondary School is a secondary education institution committed to providing learners with quality education and opportunities for personal development.

The school aims to create an environment where learners can develop academic knowledge, practical skills, discipline, creativity and responsible citizenship.

Through the use of modern teaching methods and information and communication technology, the school seeks to prepare learners for further education, employment and life in a changing world.
