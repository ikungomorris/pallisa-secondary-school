🧪 Separation of Mixtures

Learn how different physical methods are used to separate components of mixtures.

Aim

To identify suitable methods for separating different mixtures and understand the scientific principles behind each method.

Separation Methods

  • Filtration — separates an insoluble solid from a liquid.
  • Evaporation — separates a dissolved solid from a solution.
  • Distillation — separates substances based on differences in boiling points.
  • Chromatography — separates components of a mixture based on their movement through a medium.
